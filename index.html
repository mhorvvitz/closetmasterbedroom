How it was made

The SketchUp share link had no export, so the model was exported as a binary STL and parsed directly — 338,450 triangles welded into 503 connected solids, axis-aligned faces bucketed by plane, coplanar faces reassembled into rectangles. No dimension was ever read off a screenshot.

The width that fell out of the parse, 1650, was later confirmed independently by the architect’s floor plan — which also corrected the left run’s depth from 1700 to 1600.

Every deliverable here derives from one positioned-part spec. Change a number there and the cut list, drawings, assembly plan, hardware schedule, cost and render all follow. A cross-checker validates the seams between them and exits non-zero on any mismatch.
